## Checked and OK (highlights)

- **IDOR/BOLA:** all 480 routes reviewed; zero confirmed cross-user reads or
  writes. Portfolio/brokerage/Plaid/SnapTrade/Alpaca paths consistently derive
  accounts from the authenticated `user.id`; org routes consistently re-scope
  by `org_id`; judge magic-link surface re-validates room scope per call.
- **Webhooks:** Stripe (constructEvent), Alpaca (HMAC + timingSafeEqual), and
  Plaid (JWT + body-hash + iat window) all verify before trusting payloads and
  fail closed when unconfigured.
- **Cron:** all 38 cron routes + 3 scheduled endpoints require
  `Bearer CRON_SECRET` and fail closed.
- **Headers/CSP:** HSTS, nosniff, frame protection, per-request nonce CSP with
  no `unsafe-inline` scripts. Recommended (not yet added): `frame-ancestors`
  and `form-action 'self'` in the CSP.
- **CORS:** no wildcards; exact-origin allowlist, no credentialed cross-origin.
- **Cookies:** Supabase-managed auth cookies only; the single custom cookie is
  the non-sensitive theme cookie with constrained values.
- **Secrets in `src/`:** none found; server routes read provider keys from
  server-only env.
- **RLS-enabled-no-policy tables (14):** deny-all by default; accessed only via
  the service role — acceptable.
