AI ANSWERfrom 3 help articles

You can connect your brokerage account by going to your Home page, clicking + Add Portfolio, and searching for your brokerage in the Connect your brokerage grid. Select your brokerage and choose either SnapTrade (recommended) or Plaid as your connection provider. You’ll then log in to your brokerage through their secure portal, approve access, and your holdings will load into Ezana. Your connected holdings will appear in the portfolio section of your Home page, with SnapTrade syncing several times a day and Plaid updating once daily with end-of-day data.

  1. Home → + Add Portfolio
  2. Find your brokerage, pick SnapTrade or Plaid
  3. Approve access in the brokerage portal
