# Sonar 6.2: Analytical Execution and Research Fulfillment

Nov 28 2026. Builds on Sonar 6.1 (`4dec9176`, `51875943`, `a2388127`). The canonical-intent
system is unchanged in shape; 6.2 adds what runs after it.

## 1. Why the analyst eval was 72 of 253

`scripts/check-sonar-analyst-evals.mjs` runs the production router (`routeAnalysts`) on each
case in `sonar-skills/*/tests/evaluation-cases.json` and checks six things: case shape, lead
analyst, supporting analysts, boundary (analysts that must not be routed), capabilities the
plan must use, and compute. **It measures routing, not execution.** No case failed on
synthesis, citations or calculations because the harness never runs them; that is a
measurement gap, and section 5 adds the execution evals that close it.

Failure breakdown of the 181 failing cases at baseline (one primary cause per case):

| Cause | Cases | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong lead analyst | 108 | incorrect planning |
| Supporting or boundary analysts wrong | 44 | incorrect planning |
| Malformed eval case (red team asked for in standard/quick mode, support in quick mode) | 15 | eval defect |
| Required capability owned by no analyst (`committee_members`, `contractor_tickers`, `lobbying_client_tickers`, `org_research_notes`) | 10 | unsupported capability |
| Required analyst never scored | 4 | incorrect planning |
| Missing datasets, failed calculations, poor synthesis, missing citations, incomplete output | 0 measured | not measured by this harness |

Root cause of the planning failures: the lead was chosen by topical keyword scores, so a
question that names a company but asks what Congress traded in it went to equity research.
The question's *evidence demand* (what it asks the data to show) was not modelled.

## 2. What changed

### Routing (incorrect planning)
- `analysts/evidence-demand.js`: 23 typed concepts (congress trades, 13F holdings, contract
  awards, lobbying, valuation, statistical tests, sovereign debt, food security...) each with
  an owner analyst and capabilities, ordered by first mention; framing concepts (valuation,
  statistical test, competition, prediction markets) take precedence as the primary.
- `analyst-router.js`: the primary demand owner leads; other demand owners support ahead of
  topical fits; partners come from each skill's "Support when" section.
- `analysts/partners.js` is generated by `scripts/gen-sonar-partners.mjs` and drift-checked
  in `check-sonar-execution.mjs`.

### Real executors (unsupported capabilities)
All read validated public datasets through the composer; numbers are computed in code.

| Capability | Calculation | Data |
|---|---|---|
| `financial-screen` | Sector universe (GICS), filters on filed ratios first, then ranking by federal obligations, insider purchases, members of Congress buying, lobbying spend or 13F breadth; funnel with counts and companies without the data | `ticker_sectors`, `sec_fundamentals`, `company_contract_history`, `sec_insider_transactions` (code P), `congress_trades`, `lobbying_*`, 13F |
| `company-comparison` | Revenue and net income growth, gross/operating/net/FCF margin, ROE, debt to equity from filed annual figures; leaders per metric; fiscal-year alignment check | `sec_fundamentals` |
| `government-dependence` | Obligations by federal fiscal year (complete years only), growth, ratio to filed revenue, agency shares and concentration | `company_contract_history`, `sec_fundamentals` |

Canonical intent gains a `company_comparison` family ("Compare AMD, Intel and Qualcomm on
revenue growth", "How do Microsoft and Alphabet compare on free cash flow"), which used to
fall into the two-holder portfolio comparison. A screen on a measure the executor cannot
compute (P/E, short interest, ESG) or an index universe is reported unsupported and nothing
is substituted.

### Execution graph and partial results
- `runResearchComposition` takes `deadlineAt`. A step that would start after it is recorded
  as failed with `timedOut` ("Not run: the time limit was reached before this step
  started."); a running step is cut at it; finished steps are kept and reported.
- Company names in a comparison resolve server side to whole-word filer names only
  ("Intel" is INTC, never Intellicheck; "AMD" never AMDOCS). Unmatched names are listed.

### Fulfillment contracts
`composer/required-outputs.js` names the outputs each capability must produce (screen:
universe, funnel, ranked rows; comparison: two or more companies with metrics, leaders;
dependence: obligations by year, revenue ratio, agency mix; ownership, fundamentals, DCF).
A step that returns without them is `limited` and the missing outputs are named. Writer
conclusion rules per capability go into the fulfillment block.

### Confidence audit
`engine/confidence.js`: the writer's confidence is a claim. High survives only with citation
coverage of 80% or more, claim support of 80% or more, at least 3 evidence items, an audit
pass rate of 80% or more, a written answer that finished (not truncated, not the
deterministic fallback) and complete required calculations. Missing calculations, or
citation coverage under 50%, mean low. Confidence is never raised. The reasons are kept on
the run (`confidenceAudit`).

### Cache invalidation
The reuse epoch now also carries, for the two newest 13F periods, the newest refresh and
filing times and the count of amendment add-ons; for an institution, the accession and filing
time of its current rows; for a member of Congress, the newest ingestion time (a corrected
disclosure keeps its dates but gets a new row); and `OWNERSHIP_METHOD_VERSION`.

### Entitlements, RLS and quotas
Nothing is bypassed. The new analyses run only when every plan dataset they read is
entitled (`analyticsDatasets` in `registry.js`): the free plan does not include SEC
fundamentals, so guests and free users get a notice instead of a screen. Deep mode reads
every dataset, as the engine does. Daily and deep quotas are checked before any of this.

### Report UI
`CompositionReport.jsx` renders any analysis that names its columns as a table, the screen
funnel through the existing pipeline steps, and dependence tiles. Tokens only.

## 3. Results

### Analyst eval (routing)
| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Cases passing | 72 / 253 | **143 / 253** |
| Lead correct | 134 | 218 |
| Support correct | 143 | 182 |
| Required capabilities used | 124 | 189 |
| Boundary respected | 34 / 38 | 34 / 38 |

Remaining 110: support or boundary 45, lead 29 (7 ask for the methodology auditor as lead,
which the architecture does not allow), eval-case defects 15, analyst not routed 13,
unowned capabilities 8 (mostly `org_research_notes`, which only the auditor control could
own without adding a model call).

**Overfitting check.** The concepts were written while reading the eval cases, so a new
held-out set of 20 questions (`npm run test:sonar-heldout`) was written afterwards and is
never used for tuning: lead accuracy **12 / 20 before, 15 / 20 after**. The five misses
(lobbying spend phrased as "spend", a rates question about bank margins, sanctions on a
commodity, a euro-area recession, a sector momentum backtest that got no lead) are recorded
and left unfixed on purpose.

### Execution evals (new)
`npm run test:sonar-execution`, 12 tests on frozen fixtures with hand-worked numbers:
question to intent to attachment to executor to fulfillment for a two-stage screen, a
three-company comparison and a dependence read; failures for one company and no contract
history; the plan gate; the confidence rules; deadline recovery; name resolution; partners
drift. All pass.

### Real data spot check (read-only SQL, production)
Palantir government dependence, recomputed by hand from the same tables: federal obligations
FY2026 $1.340B vs FY2025 $1.021B (+31.3%); filed revenue FY2025 $4.475B, so obligations are
29.9% of revenue (an exposure indicator, and the fiscal years differ, which the report
states); Department of Defense 73.0% of FY2026 obligations. Energy screen universe: 212
tickers, 132 with filed annual revenue in the window.

### Production runs (`sonar_research_runs`, last 14 days)
| | Runs | Written answer | Deterministic fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before 6.1 writer fix | 21 | 5 | 16 |
| After 6.1 (Nov 28, from 11:55 UTC) | 2 | 2 | 0 |

Latency: deterministic runs averaged 26.8 s, written runs 23.7 s (standard depth). The
after-6.1 sample is two runs; it shows the fix working, not a rate. Two of seven written runs
were stated "high"; under the 6.2 audit a run is high only if its stored coverage meets the
thresholds, which the next runs record in `confidenceAudit`.

### Model costs
Not measurable for runs before `1ce67edb` (per-call usage capture landed there today). From
that commit each call records requested and served model, tokens, searches and latency;
6.2 adds no model calls (all three executors are code). The standard writer budget stays
2,400 output tokens.

## 4. Production verification

Deployed with `ee8afd13`. The new analyses need a plan that includes SEC fundamentals, so
they cannot be verified as a guest, and the guest ping limit in the test browser was already
reached; neither limit was bypassed. To verify, sign in on a tier 1+ account and run:

1. "Which energy companies with operating margins above 15% did insiders buy the most over the last 90 days?"
2. "Compare AMD, Intel and Qualcomm on revenue growth and operating margin"
3. "How dependent is Palantir on government revenue?"
4. "Find bank stocks with a P/E below 10." (expect the unsupported notice, no screen)

Each should show the calculated table (and funnel or tiles) with the required-outputs list.

## 5. Remaining unsupported or partial

- Screens: P/E, short interest, ESG, index membership (S&P 500), sub-industries
  (semiconductors, banks, defense, retail, airlines run on the broader GICS sector, which is
  stated).
- Ownership percentage of shares outstanding, ascending ownership rankings, recent-trade-only
  ownership filters (unchanged from 6.1).
- Analyst-eval capabilities with no owner: `org_research_notes`, `committee_members`.
- Macro, valuation (beyond the DCF) and quant families still run through the engine's
  evidence and writer, not a dedicated executor; no new executor is claimed for them.
- Pre-existing: the 13F ownership ranking runs for guests although `13f` is a tier 2
  dataset. Left as is to preserve current behaviour; flagged for a decision.
